Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users can delete their account and associated personal data for our mobile application.

Delete account within the app

Users can delete their account directly in the app by navigating to:

Me → Settings → Delete Account

Once confirmed, the account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted.

Request account deletion via email

Alternatively, users may request account and data deletion by contacting us via email:

📧 appassociationdapp@gmail.com

Please include the registered email address or username when submitting the request.

Data deletion timeline

Account deletion requests will be processed within 7 business days after verification.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account information (email, username)
  • User profile data
  • App-related usage data

Data retention

No personal data will be retained after account deletion, except where required by applicable laws or regulations.
